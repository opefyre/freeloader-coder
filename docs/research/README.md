## Research question

What must a local autonomous development product do for a GitHub-capable
builder to trust it with meaningful work without requiring platform-engineering
knowledge?

## Evidence classes

1. **Observed workflow evidence** — recurring behavior and failure reactions
   observed during the Household prototype program.
2. **Published research** — current primary-source developer surveys and
   delivery research.
3. **Product hypotheses** — explicit assumptions that remain subject to
   usability and longitudinal validation.

The repository never labels a hypothesis as validated evidence.

## Research limitations

- The direct observational evidence is deep but comes from one continuing
  founder/operator workflow. It is representative of the initial target profile
  but not statistically representative of the market.
- Broad developer surveys overrepresent professional developers relative to
  low-code and no-code builders.
- Public evidence supports the trust and verification problem more strongly
  than it validates the exact packaging or interaction design.
- Before Connected Beta, test setup, recovery, and review flows with at least
  five external GitHub-capable builders across macOS, Windows/WSL, and Linux.

These limitations do not block the Foundation contract. They do block claims
about market-wide usability or conversion.
